# Kakraba Personal Website: Build Plan

The plan for Dr. Samuel Kakraba's **personal portfolio website**: one place where anyone can learn who he is, what he works on, and what he has achieved.

> **Scope:** this is a site about *him*, separate from the lab site (`professor-samuel-project`). The lab site keeps the group, students, recruiting and applications. This site links to it.

---

## 1. The big picture

**Purpose:** a visitor should understand who Dr. Kakraba is within 60 seconds, then be able to go deeper into any part of his work.

**Who visits:**
1. Academics, collaborators and search committees: *"What does he work on, and how strong is it?"*
2. Event organizers and media: *"Bio, photo, speaking topics, contact?"*
3. Students and the general public: *"Who is he, and where is the lab?"* (short answer here, then a link to the lab site)

**Tagline (pick one):**
- "Building trustworthy, accessible AI for public health, from New Orleans to Accra." (recommended)
- "Explainable AI for healthier populations, from molecules to communities."

**Three things every visitor should leave knowing:**
1. He builds **explainable, open AI tools** for public health. **SMART-Pred** is the proof.
2. His research runs **from molecules to populations**, in four areas.
3. His career runs **from teaching mathematics in Ghana to a professorship at Tulane**, and he remains a bridge between US and African health data science.

## 5. Design direction

**Feel:** personal, warm and credible, like a well-designed magazine profile rather than a lab dashboard. The person comes first: big photo, his voice, his story.

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Colors** | Off-white background · near-black text · **deep green** primary · **warm gold** accent (a nod to Ghana). Include a dark mode. |
| **Typography** | Serif headings (*Fraunces* or *Source Serif 4*) + clean sans body (*Inter*) |
| **Signature visuals** | Career timeline/map on About · subtle graph-network lines in the hero (a nod to his graph theory work) |
| **Imagery** | Real photos: headshot, teaching, speaking, Ghana visits. Figures from his papers as thumbnails. No stock "AI brain" images. |
| **Layout** | Lots of whitespace, ~1100px max width, simple cards |
| **Motion** | Light only: number counters, fade-in on scroll |

**Make it look different from the lab site**, so the two don't feel like duplicates. They can share colors so they still feel related.
